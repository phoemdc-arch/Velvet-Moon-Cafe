The Moonlit Beginning

A dream born under quiet stars

Founded in 2019, Velvet Moon Café was born out of a simple desire: to create a cozy, twilight haven for night owls, creatives, and wanderers seeking solace from the fast-paced world. While traditional coffee shops close as dusk settles, our doors open to welcome the stillness of the night.

Every elixir we serve is thoughtful—from ethically sourced shade-grown beans to botanical infusions made from fresh lavender, cardamoms, and rare blossoms. We believe coffee is more than caffeine; it is a ritual of comfort and connection.
